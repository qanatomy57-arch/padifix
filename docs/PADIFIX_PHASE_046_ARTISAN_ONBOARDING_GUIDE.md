## 1. Welcome to PadiFix: Wetin Be PadiFix?

**PadiFix** is an online trade directory and marketplace made specifically for Nigerian artisans, technicians, and skilled professionals. 

Whether you are an **Electrician, Plumber, AC Repairer, Generator Technician, Welder, Carpenter, Painter, Mechanic, or Beauty Specialist**, PadiFix helps people living in your area find your business when they need urgent or scheduled work done.

### The 3 Core Truths of PadiFix:
1. **0% Commission on Your Hard Work:** PadiFix is NOT an agency. We do not deduct 10%, 15%, or 20% from your workmanship. Whatever price you agree with the customer is 100% yours.
2. **Direct Call & WhatsApp Connection:** Customers reach you directly on your phone or WhatsApp. No third-party agents in the middle.
3. **You Control Your Payments:** Customers pay you directly into your bank account or cash, exactly how you choose to arrange it with them. PadiFix does not hold your money in escrow.

## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Register & Create Your Profile

Joining PadiFix takes less than 3 minutes on any smartphone:

### Step 1: Open the Registration Page
Visit **`https://padifix.vercel.app/register.html`** on your phone's browser (Chrome, Safari, or Edge).

### Step 2: Choose Your Account Type & Enter Details
- Select **"I am a Service Provider / Artisan"**.
- Enter your real **Full Name** and **Business Name** (e.g., *Sunday Okon Electric & Solar Works*).
- Enter your valid **Email Address** and a secure **Password** (at least 6 characters).
- Enter your direct **Phone Number** and **WhatsApp Number**.

### Step 3: Select Your Trade & Local Government Area (LGA)
- Pick your primary trade category (e.g., *Generator Repair*, *Plumbing*, *Welding*).
- Select your **State** (e.g., *Delta State*, *Lagos State*) and your exact **LGA / Area** (e.g., *Warri South*, *Effurun / Uvwie*, *Ikeja*).
- Add other skills you have (e.g., an AC technician can also list refrigerator and freezer repairs).

### Step 4: Add Your Service Description
Write a simple, honest description of what you do:
> *"Master electrician with 8 years experience in residential wiring, inverter installation, solar battery maintenance, and emergency electrical faults in Effurun and Warri. Fast response and clean job guaranteed."*

### Step 5: Save & View Your Live Profile
Once submitted, you are live on PadiFix! You will receive a direct link to your public profile page that you can share with your customers.

## 3. How Customers Find & Contact You

When someone in your community needs your service:
1. They search for your skill (e.g., *"Generator mechanic in Warri"*) on `https://padifix.vercel.app`.
2. They see your profile card showing your trade, area, and experience.
3. They tap **"Call"** or **"Chat on WhatsApp"**.
4. A pre-written inquiry opens with their specific service need and location.
5. You discuss the problem, state your diagnostic or service price, and arrange a visit time.

---

## 4. How Payments Work: 100% Direct

* **PadiFix does not collect customer job money.**
* You negotiate your price directly with the client.
* You agree on inspection fees, material costs, and workmanship charges.
* You collect your payment via cash, bank transfer (USSD/Mobile App), or POS directly from the client.
* PadiFix charges zero transaction fees on any job you complete.

## 5. Reputation & Customer Reviews: How to Grow Your Trust

On PadiFix, your reputation is your greatest asset. When you do a great job, make sure you collect a verified review:

1. After completing the job, open your **Provider Dashboard** (`https://padifix.vercel.app/dashboard.html`).
2. Find the customer inquiry in your **Leads & Contacts** tab.
3. Tap **"Request Review via WhatsApp"**.
4. PadiFix generates a unique, secure review link for that specific customer.
5. When the customer taps the link and rates you (1 to 5 stars), it immediately appears on your public profile with an official **"Verified Customer"** mark.
6. **No fake reviews:** PadiFix strictly blocks self-reviews and fake ratings. Real reviews mean real trust.

## 6. Verification: What is the PadiFix "Verified" Badge?

A **Verified** badge tells customers that PadiFix Compliance Desk has physically reviewed your government identification (NIN slip, Driver's License, Voter's Card, or Passport).

### Verification Rules:
* Verification is **one-time and permanent** per account. Once approved, you never have to resubmit documents again.
* Verification is available to active subscribers on our Basic, Pro, or Premium plans.
* To apply, open your dashboard, tap **"Submit Verification"**, select your ID type, enter your ID number, and upload a clear photo of the document.
* PadiFix Compliance Desk reviews submissions within 24 to 48 hours.
* All verification documents are stored in an encrypted private vault and are **never** shown to the public or customers.

## 8. Safety & Professional Guidelines for Artisans

* **Price Clarity:** Always inform the client upfront if you charge a diagnostic or mobility inspection fee before touching equipment.
* **Safe Locations:** For new clients, meet at their registered home or business address during daylight hours whenever possible.
* **Respectful Communication:** Respond promptly to customer WhatsApp inquiries. If you are busy on another site, send a quick polite message: *"I am currently finishing a job in Effurun, I will call you back by 3 PM."*
* **Quality Materials:** Recommend genuine parts to clients to prevent complaints and earn 5-star ratings.
