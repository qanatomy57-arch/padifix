# PadiFix — Customer Pilot Guide: How to Find & Hire Reliable Local Artisans

**Platform Origin:** `https://padifix.vercel.app`  
**Target Audience:** Customers, Homeowners, Facility Managers & Small Businesses in Nigeria  
**Coverage Scope:** Nationwide Directory with Initial Focus on Warri, Effurun & Surrounding Delta Communities  

---

## 1. What is PadiFix for Customers?

Finding a skilled, trustworthy technician in Nigeria often relies on stressful word-of-mouth recommendations. **PadiFix** gives you direct access to local artisans—electricians, plumbers, generator mechanics, AC technicians, carpenters, painters, and more—operating right in your neighborhood.

### Important Principles to Know:
* **Direct Access:** You can call or chat with artisans on WhatsApp without paying any booking or platform search fees.
* **No Middleman Markups:** PadiFix does not add commissions or middleman margins to your quote. You negotiate the job cost directly with the artisan.
* **Direct Payments:** PadiFix is not an escrow service. You pay the artisan directly using cash, USSD, or mobile bank transfer upon agreement.

---

## 2. Step-by-Step: How to Find & Hire an Artisan

### Step 1: Search Your Need
1. Go to **`https://padifix.vercel.app/search.html`**.
2. Type what you need in plain everyday language:
   - *"Electrician to fix tripped breaker"*
   - *"Plumber near Effurun"*
   - *"Generator mechanic in Warri"*
3. Or select a category from the dropdown (e.g., *Plumbing*, *HVAC / AC*, *Generator Repair*).

### Step 2: Filter by Location & Verification
* Select your **State** and **LGA** to find specialists closest to you.
* Check **"Verified Pros Only"** to see artisans whose government identification has been formally vetted by the PadiFix Compliance Desk.

### Step 3: Review the Artisan's Profile
Click on any artisan's card to view their full profile:
* **Trade Title & Specializations:** Specific equipment they repair or install.
* **Service Areas:** Neighborhoods they travel to for site visits.
* **Experience & Badges:** Years in practice and verification status.
* **Customer Reputation:** Genuine ratings and reviews from previous clients.
* **"New Artisan" Tag:** Artisans who recently joined and haven't accumulated reviews yet display a clear "New Artisan" status. We do not invent artificial 5-star ratings.

### Step 4: Contact via Call or WhatsApp
* Click **"Call"** to phone them immediately for urgent faults.
* Click **"Chat on WhatsApp"** to send details of the issue. You can select urgency level (*Urgent*, *2-3 Days*, *Flexible*) and attach photos of the broken appliance or site.

## 3. How Payment Works: Direct & Transparent

1. **Inspection / Mobility Fee:** If the artisan needs to travel to inspect the fault before quoting, agree on whether an inspection fee applies before they dispatch.
2. **Workmanship & Materials:** Request a clear breakdown of parts/materials versus workmanship labor.
3. **Payment Method:** Pay the artisan directly via cash, mobile banking app transfer, or USSD upon completion or agreed milestone.
4. **No PadiFix Checkout:** PadiFix will **never** ask you to pay into an escrow account or enter credit card numbers to book an artisan.

---

## 4. Understanding Trust & Badging on PadiFix

### The Unified "Verified" Badge (🛡️ Verified)
When you see the green shield with the word **"Verified"**:
* The artisan's official Nigerian Government Identification (NIN slip, Driver's License, Voter's Card, or International Passport) has been physically examined and approved by our Compliance Desk.
* Their trade category and business information have been validated.
* PadiFix never exposes private ID numbers or documents to the public.

---

## 5. Leaving a Genuine Review After Job Completion

Your honest feedback helps your neighbors find great professionals and rewards quality workmanship:

1. When the job is done, ask the artisan to send you their **PadiFix Review Invitation** on WhatsApp.
2. Tap the link sent to your phone (this is a secure, single-use link created just for your completed interaction).
3. Select a rating from **1 to 5 stars** and optionally leave comments detailing their punctuality, skill, and honesty.
4. Your review is published instantly with an official **"Verified Customer"** badge.

---

## 6. Practical Safety Tips for Hiring Service Providers

1. **Verify Identity:** When the artisan arrives, confirm their name matches their PadiFix profile.
2. **Agree on Price First:** Never allow work to commence without agreeing on the total labor fee and who purchases replacement materials.
3. **Receipts & Invoices:** For major equipment purchases (e.g., compressor, solar inverter, pump), request the original vendor receipt and warranty card.
4. **Daylight Hours:** Schedule first-time home repairs during daytime hours whenever feasible.
5. **Report Suspicious Behavior:** If any provider acts unprofessionally or misrepresents their identity, report them immediately to PadiFix Support (`support@padifix.ng`).
