# PadiFix Phase 045 — Real-World Artisan Onboarding & Controlled Pilot Guide

## Purpose
This document specifies the operational framework, quality standards, and acquisition procedures for onboarding PadiFix's **first genuine Nigerian artisans and customers**. 

PadiFix rejects artificial liquidity, bot seeding, fabricated reviews, and fake provider profiles. Sustainable trust in the Nigerian artisan marketplace is earned solely through reachable, skilled, and honest professionals doing genuine work for real clients.

---

## 1. Provider Acquisition Strategy

### Channels for Genuine Supply
Outreach must engage real-world skilled hands through trusted, high-touch relationships:

1. **Local Trade & Artisan Associations**:
   - Nigerian Association of Electrical Technicians (NAET)
   - Association of Professional Carpenters & Furniture Makers
   - Plumbers & Mechanics Trade Unions across key hubs (Lagos, Abuja, Port Harcourt, Warri)
2. **Community WhatsApp Hubs**:
   - Estate residents' groups, trade guild broadcast channels, and local business network forums.
3. **Personal & Professional Referrals**:
   - Vetted mechanics, electricians, tailors, and builders with proven, direct track records known to team members and trusted early adopters.
4. **Direct Field Outreach (Artisan Workshops)**:
   - In-person visits to established workshops, cluster markets (e.g. Alaba, Ladipo, Somolu, Okpe, Ikeja), explaining PadiFix's 0% commission direct-contact model.

> [!NOTE]
> No outreach is represented as completed yet. These channels form the execution roadmap for post-Phase 045 operations.

## 2. Artisan Onboarding Checklist

When a genuine provider registers on `https://padifix.vercel.app/register.html`, they must be guided to provide:

- [ ] **Real Identity**: Genuine full name, business name, and personal headshot/avatar.
- [ ] **Trade Taxonomy**: Accurate primary trade selection from canonical 15 Nigerian categories (e.g. Electrician, Plumber, Tailor, AC Specialist, Mason, Carpenter).
- [ ] **Verifiable Location**: Exact State, LGA (from Nigeria's authoritative 774 LGA catalog), and neighborhood/locality.
- [ ] **Direct Contact**: Genuine, active Nigerian phone number and reachable WhatsApp number (in E.164 / `234...` format).
- [ ] **Clear Service Description**: Concise, honest summary of craft specialties, warranties, and scope of work.
- [ ] **Authentic Proof of Work**: Real photographs of completed projects, workshops, or installations (no stock photos or borrowed imagery).
- [ ] **Pricing Transparency**: Realistic starting rate or inspection consultation fee.
- [ ] **Optional Compliance Verification**: Submission of government-issued identification (NIN Slip, Voter's Card, Driver's License, International Passport, or CAC Certificate) for the PadiFix Compliance Desk review.

## 3. Quality Standard & Vetting Filters

PadiFix optimizes for **reliability and reputation over raw provider count**:

1. **Reachable Guarantee**:
   - Phone call & WhatsApp ping test conducted before featuring or recommending an artisan.
2. **Zero Fabricated Metrics**:
   - All newly onboarded providers start with `rating = 0.0`, `reviews_count = 0`, `completed_jobs = 0`, and display `"New Artisan (No reviews yet)"`.
   - No star ratings appear until a real customer writes a review.
3. **Transparent Commercials**:
   - 0% customer commission.
   - PadiFix never holds client job payments or operates escrow.
   - Customers pay artisans directly upon milestone completion or inspection.

## 4. Controlled Customer Pilot Workflow

To validate the end-to-end user loop under real-world conditions:

```
[1. Discovery] Customer searches by trade & LGA on padifix.vercel.app
       ↓
[2. Profile Inspection] Customer reads verified credentials, trade details, and service area
       ↓
[3. Direct Contact] Customer initiates inquiry via WhatsApp Intake Modal or Call Button
       ↓
[4. Engagement] Customer and artisan agree on scope, timeline, and direct payment terms
       ↓
[5. Service Delivery] Artisan performs job; customer inspects and settles directly
       ↓
[6. Review Invitation] Artisan clicks "⭐ Request Review" in Dashboard to issue signed token
       ↓
[7. Verified Review] Customer clicks deep-link and submits rating → "🛡️ Verified Customer" badge
```

## 5. Support & Safety Infrastructure

New users and artisans can report issues or seek assistance through:
- **Direct Support Channel**: `support@padifix.vercel.app` / WhatsApp Support Helpdesk.
- **Reporting Mechanism**: Report an artisan or suspicious activity directly via the provider profile page.
- **Clear Disclaimers**: Public trust notices highlighting that PadiFix provides directory matching and ID vetting, but does not arbitrate offline physical agreements or hold customer deposits.
